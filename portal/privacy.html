PRIVACY

Privacy notice for email updates

Last updated 25 November 2026

This notice explains how Gifting Needs handles your details when you sign up for our email updates.

Who we are

Gifting Needs is a corporate gifting business in Bengaluru, India. For any question about your details, email info@giftingneeds.org or call +91 63610 54099.

What we collect

When you sign up:

  • your email address, and your name if you give it
  • a record of your consent: the wording you agreed to, the page you signed up on, the date and time, your IP address and your browser

When you confirm by email: the date, time and IP address of your confirmation.

When we email you: whether each email was delivered, bounced or reported as spam, and whether you unsubscribed. We do not track whether you open our emails or click their links.

Why we use it

  • To send you emails about our corporate gifting collections and offers, which you asked for.
  • To keep a record that you asked for them.
  • To stop emailing addresses that unsubscribe, bounce or report us as spam.

You are only added after you confirm by email. We never buy, rent or share email lists, and we do not sell your details.

Who helps us

MailBurst (mailburst.in) runs our signup form and sends our emails for us. Emails are delivered through Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES) in Australia, and subscriber records are stored by MailBurst’s database provider, Supabase. Your details may therefore be processed outside India.

How long we keep it

While you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we keep your email address and your consent record so that we never email you again. If you never confirm your signup, you will not receive any emails from us.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of every email.
  • To see, correct or delete your details, email info@giftingneeds.org.
