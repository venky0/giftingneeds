Good to know

Questions we hear often

What kinds of events do you manage?

Corporate conferences and townhalls, product launches and brand activations, dealer meets and awards nights, festive and employee celebrations, and roadshows or exhibition stalls. If your event is not on this list, tell us about it. We will tell you honestly whether we are the right team.

Do you handle everything end to end?

Yes. We can take a brief from concept to the final applause: venue, staging, LED, sound and light, anchors and artists, registration, hospitality and on-ground management. You can also hire us for just one part, such as production or a live activation.

How early should we contact you?

As early as you can. For larger events, a few weeks gives the best choice of venues, vendors and artists. Shorter timelines are often possible too, so share your date and we will tell you what is realistic.

How is an event priced?

Every event is quoted against your brief: guest count, venue, production needs, duration and city. You receive an itemised quotation, so you can see where each rupee goes and choose where to spend more or less.

Can you also supply gifts, trophies and event merchandise?

Yes, and this is where we are different. Gifting Needs is our core business, so branded welcome kits, hampers, awards, trophies and giveaways come from the same team, in the same brand look, delivered to the venue.

Do you work outside Bengaluru?

We are based in Bengaluru and plan events across India. Share your city and dates in the enquiry form and we will confirm the logistics.
